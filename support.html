Common questions

How do credits work? Your first analysis is free. After that, buy analyses as you go, or subscribe to Premium for unlimited analyses plus the full toolkit (rewrites, compare, Ask-AI, missing protections, PDF export).

How do I restore purchases? Open the Premium screen and tap "Restore purchases".

Is this legal advice? No — Versed gives general, educational information and isn't a substitute for a qualified professional.
